Privacy and Terms

Version 2026-10 · Last updated 26 October 2026

Worker Buddy is an app that connects daily-wage workers with the people who hire them, works out a fair wage, and keeps a record of each job and payment. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect and why

  • Account: your mobile number or Google email when you sign in, so you can get your account back on a new phone and so fake accounts are kept out.
  • Profile: name, mobile number, type of work, years of experience, ITI number (optional), birth year (workers, to check you are 18 or older), home state and city (optional), tools, UPI ID (optional, so you can be paid).
  • Jobs and payments: each job's place, hours, agreed wage, start and finish times, overtime, payment and your confirmation, messages, materials and ratings. This is your wage record and pay slip.
  • Location: while a worker is online or on a job, the phone's location, to show nearby jobs, the way to the site and the arrival time. The place where the worker started work is kept as evidence for disputes.
  • Crew book (contractors): names, phone numbers and attendance of crew members you add. Add them only with their permission.
  • Safety: heat reports, and reports you make about another person.

2. Who can see it

  • Your mobile number, UPI ID and exact location are shown only to the other person on a job you have both agreed to, and live location only while that job is on.
  • Other people see your name, type of work, ratings, jobs done and an area rounded to about 1 km.
  • Employer record check: a signed-in worker who types an employer's mobile number sees that employer's record on Worker Buddy (jobs, paying on time, ratings, "not paid" reports, unpaid jobs and the welfare tags workers gave). The employer's name, number and place are never shown. Each worker can check 30 numbers a day; the time of each check is kept for 2 days to apply that limit, then deleted.
  • My own jobs (worker app, Earnings): jobs you write down from outside the app stay only on your phone and are never sent to us.
  • The Worker Buddy team sees reports and may look at a job's record to settle a report or dispute.
  • We use service providers to run the app: Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), Google (sign-in), an SMS provider (sign-in codes), OpenStreetMap (maps and nearby places) and Open-Meteo (weather). They process data only to provide their service.
  • We never sell your data and never share it for advertising. We share it with the police or a court only when the law requires it.

3. How long we keep it

  • Your profile and wage record stay while your account is open, so your pay slips keep working.
  • The place where work started is wiped 180 days after the job. A worker's last location is wiped after 90 days offline. Heat-report locations are wiped after a year.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, crew list and saved places are removed. Jobs stay in the other person's record (their pay slip), with your name and number removed.

4. Your rights

  • See and download your data: 👤 account button → Download my data.
  • Correct it: edit your profile in the app, or write to us.
  • Delete it and withdraw consent: 👤 account button → Delete my account. Finish or cancel any job in progress first.
  • Complain to our grievance officer (below). If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

5. Children

Workers must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, each account can change only its own jobs, and private details are locked away from other users. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Terms of use

  • What Worker Buddy is: a tool to find work, agree a wage and keep a record. Worker Buddy is not the employer and does not hold or pay wages. The person who hires pays the worker directly (cash or UPI).
  • Fair pay: the fair-wage estimate is guidance. The person who hires must pay at least the legal minimum wage, and overtime at twice the normal rate.
  • Honesty: post only real jobs, give true details, and don't abuse, threaten or cheat anyone. Workers must be 18 or older.
  • Safety: report anything wrong with the ⚠️ Report button. In danger, call 112 first.
  • Suspension: we can put an account on hold or close it for fake jobs, non-payment, abuse or reports from several people.
  • Disputes are between the worker and the person who hired them. Worker Buddy gives both the job's evidence page and can help, but is not a party to the agreement.
  • No guarantee: the app is provided as it is. We are not responsible for work done, injuries or losses between users, except where the law says otherwise.
  • These terms follow the laws of India. Courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, have jurisdiction.
