Choose your app

👷 Worker app Get jobs nearby or from WhatsApp, hear them read aloud, start with an OTP, and get a pay slip. Open 🏠 Employer app Post a job at a fair rate, share it on WhatsApp, and nearby workers get it instantly. Open

How it works

  1. Post job: type of work, place and hours
  2. Fair rate calculated and shown before anyone accepts
  3. Shared on WhatsApp, and nearby workers’ phones ring
  4. Worker accepts and starts with an OTP
  5. Work with heat alerts, water breaks and overtime
  6. Employer pays, worker confirms: pay slip and rating
🗺️ Wage map: what people are paid near you

💡 Live demo: open the Worker app on one phone and the Employer app on another. A booking rings on the worker’s phone within a second.
